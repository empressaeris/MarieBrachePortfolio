About

Marie Brache is a digital media artist and designer based in Oviedo, Florida, whose work moves between two worlds. Such as vibrant character illustration rooted in the role-playing games she loves to practical design that makes technology easier to understand. Her illustrations, from portraits of heroes and adventurers with clean linework and flat, cell-style color with gradient backgrounds. Her design work, from event flyers to instructional booklets, favors strong geometric layouts and high-contrast palettes built in Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator. Her 3D and game work draws on Autodesk 3Ds Max and Unreal Engine.

As a professional artist starting in 2017, Marie built a wide-ranging career at the University of Central Florida as a graphic design assistant, an IT client support specialist and currently, an administrative assistant. Along the way, she has designed covers for two editions of the Guide to Teaching at UCF, organized faculty events and conferences of up to 200 participants and created a 22-page visual guide that helped prepare faculty for the university’s move to Canvas New Quizzes. She currently works at UCF as of 2026.

Her personal art is inspired by Final Fantasy, Chrono Trigger, The Legend of Zelda and Elder Scrolls, Marie is passionate about telling stories through characters and the worlds they inhabit. She holds a B.A. in Digital Media - Game Design from UCF and is completing her M.A. in Digital Media. She is developing an original turn-based role-playing game in Unreal Engine that combines low-poly 3D environments with detailed 2D character art.
