mbrache@protonmail.com · Oviedo, FL

Born and raised in Orlando, Marie Brache is a digital media artist and designer with a B.A. in Digital Media – Game Design and a career at the University of Central Florida that began in 2017. Her work spans print and instructional design, digital character illustration and game art, and she is currently completing a Master of Arts in Digital Media.
